## Extension Path

The DB will be extended successively across further domains/sources (test_db grows beyond the 7 reference papers). After each extension, re-eval against the larger test collection to check whether the baseline config (CC α=0.8, rerank=False default, top_k=12, Qwen3-Embedding-8B + SPLADE-v3) stays stable under a changed data distribution.

Expected phases:

1. **Current baseline** (n=250 chunks, 17 queries) — structural config validation on RAG-internal methodology material.
2. **Extension 1** (test_db + X papers/domains outside RAG methodology) — check domain stability: does the baseline config break when switching to other content classes?
3. **Extension 2** (test_db again + X) — scaling stability: does anything break between ~250 and ~1000 chunks?
4. **Sweep re-runs** at each extension point for alpha, top_k, rerank on/off — check whether optimal values shift with data scale/domain.

Goal: empirical evidence that the baseline choice is not overfit to the small initial DB. If, e.g., α=0.8 is optimal at n=250 but shifts to α=0.6 at n=1000, that is an important signal.

## Open Questions

- Worker-generated queries vs hand-curated ground truth — quality threshold for the authoritative eval. Worker queries skew heavily factual and very specific (clear substring hits) — could artificially inflate NDCG since snippets are near-exact-match.
- Graded relevance (rel ∈ {0,1,2,3}) instead of binary for NDCG — snippet match as rel=2, expected_document without snippet as rel=1, rest rel=0. Sharper metric but more curation effort.
- Statistical significance (paired t-test) for sweep deltas at n=17 — power limit, small effects (~2-3pp) likely not distinguishable from 0.
- Latency tracking per query — the reranker trade-off question remains unresolved.
